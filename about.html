About NOOR

NOOR is an Islamic companion designed to support your daily connection with the Qur’an and Islamic learning.

Explore Qur’an reading, search verses, listen to recitation, and save your favourite passages in one simple place.

Our Purpose

To make Qur’an access and beneficial Islamic learning easier, wherever you are.

May Allah accept this effort and make it beneficial for everyone. Ameen.
